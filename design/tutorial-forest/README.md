The Four Design Pillars of the SkogRPG Cyberpunk Aesthetic
Nordic Solarpunk Meets Gritty Cyber-Sprawl ("Skog" First)

Organic Canopy & Synthetic Infrastructure: Heavy Scandinavian pine and spruce evergreens (skog) are not merely backdrops; they anchor the entire scene. Power cables, server chassis, and fiber conduits weave directly through roots and bark.

Bioluminescent Currents: Streams and natural pathways double as glowing data channels (bright cyan conduits slicing through damp forest floor and wild grass).

The "Cold Teal & Electric Magenta" Palette

Shadows & Atmosphere: Dominated by deep forest pines, midnight blues, slate grays, and muted purples.

Accent Glow: Sharp, uncompressed 16-bit neon—specifically electric mint/cyan (#00FFAA/#00FFFF), glowing lavender, and synthwave magenta (#FF007F) on signage, terminals, and visor optics.

In-World Diegetic HUD & JRPG Framework

Chunky Hardware Overlays: Party status cards, HP/EN meters, and terminal screens exist right in the playable frame, evoking mid-90s PC and Super Famicom adventure UI.

Micro-Task Quest Framing: Side quests and everyday actions (debugging routines, autonomous agent tasks, system loops) render as crisp pixel checklists and terminal logs.

Co-Op Companions: Human Hackers & Cyber-Fauna

Party Synergy: Cyber-augmented operators in jackets and visor HUDs work shoulder-to-shoulder with robotic forest wildlife (cyber-wolves, drone familiars) and modular assistant bots.

Tactile Terminals in the Wild: Workbenches, CRTs, and patch panels sit exposed to the elements, making routine automation feel like an expedition outpost.
